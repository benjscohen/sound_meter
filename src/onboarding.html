Know when you’re getting loud.

Sound Meter is a small orb that floats over your calls. Green when you’re comfortable, red when you’re too loud.

Let it hear you

Sound Meter measures how loud you are and nothing else. Audio is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

Now say something

Ignore your speakers

Music or a loud call shouldn’t count as you. Sound Meter can subtract whatever your Mac is playing, so only your voice moves the orb.

You’re all set

The orb will appear at the top right of your screen.

  • Click the orb to see how your day sounded.
  • Drag it anywhere. It floats above every window.
  • Hover over it for Settings, or right-click for the menu.
