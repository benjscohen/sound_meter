Know when you’re getting loud.

Sound Meter is a small orb that floats above your work. It stays green while you’re speaking comfortably and warms to red when you’re not.

Allow microphone access

Sound Meter only measures how loud you are. Audio is never recorded, stored, or sent anywhere — everything happens on this Mac.

Tune it to your voice

Say a sentence the way you’d normally talk. Leave it on Auto and Sound Meter tunes itself to your voice, or pick the setting where the orb stays green.

You’re all set

  • Click the orb to see how your day sounded.
  • Drag it anywhere. It floats above every window.
  • Hover over it for Settings, or right-click for the menu.
