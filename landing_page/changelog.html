Changelog

What’s new in Sound Meter.

Sound Meter updates itself. This is what changed along the way.

Tunes itself, and stays out of the way

1.4.0November 2026

  • Automatic sensitivity. Sound Meter learns your normal speaking voice from less than a minute of talking, so there’s nothing to pick. You can still choose a level by hand, or ask it to relearn.
  • Listen only during calls. The orb can hide, and release the microphone, whenever no other app is using it.
  • Orb preferences. Three sizes, three opacities, and optional level symbols so the orb reads without relying on colour.
  • Notifications, if you want them. Both are off unless you turn them on: a gentle nudge after you’ve been loud for a while, and a weekly note when your week is ready to see.
  • Smaller downloads. Separate builds for Apple silicon and Intel Macs, each about half the size.

Sharing, done properly

1.3.0November 10, 2026

  • Share links. A personal link that shows your week’s card wherever you paste it. The numbers travel in the link itself; nothing is stored online.
  • One-click posting to X, LinkedIn, Threads, Bluesky, WhatsApp and email, plus the macOS share sheet.
  • Copy or save the card as an image.

Meetings, people and patterns

1.2.0November 10, 2026

  • Meetings and people. Connect your calendar to see how loud you were in each meeting, and who you tend to be loud with. Events are read on your Mac and never stored.
  • Call apps. Each minute is credited to the app you were on a call with.
  • Trends. A weekly summary, a calm streak, and a heatmap of when you get loud.
  • An explicit Options button appears when you hover the orb.

A new Sound Meter

1.1.0November 10, 2026

  • Redesigned orb: smoother, click-through around the edges, and it remembers where you put it.
  • A guided first run that handles microphone access and calibration.
  • Insights. A private, per-minute history of how loud you’ve been, with CSV export.
  • One click on the orb for microphone, sensitivity, pause and more.
  • Runs natively on Apple silicon as well as Intel.

The first releases

1.0November 2025

  • The floating orb that grows and turns from green to red as you get louder.
  • Five sensitivity levels and a microphone picker.
  • A menu bar icon, faster start-up, and automatic updates.
