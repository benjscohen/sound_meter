How it works

One glance tells you.

No numbers, no graphs to read mid-sentence. The orb grows and changes colour with your voice, right at the edge of your vision.

Sounding good

Small and green. You’re at a comfortable level, so you can forget it’s there.

Getting loud

It swells and turns amber as you get animated. A gentle nudge, not an alarm.

Too loud

Big and red. Take a breath, bring it down, and watch it settle back to green.

Built for calls

Small on purpose.

Sound Meter does one thing and stays out of the way while it does it.

Floats above everything

Stays visible over every window and full-screen app, including your video calls and slides.

Private by design

It measures volume and nothing else. No audio is recorded, stored, or sent anywhere. Ever.

Tuned to you

Five sensitivity levels, from a whisper-quiet room to a noisy open office or a hot mic.

Any microphone

Built-in mic, AirPods, or a studio setup. Pick the one you’re using and switch any time.

Put it anywhere

Drag the orb to whichever corner of the screen suits you. It’s also in your menu bar when you need it.

Updates itself

New versions install quietly in the background. Signed and notarized by Apple.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does it record or listen to what I say?

No. Sound Meter only measures how loud the microphone signal is, moment to moment, on your Mac. No audio is recorded, stored, or sent anywhere. The app is open source, so you can read exactly what it does, and the privacy page spells it out.

Does it work with Zoom, Google Meet and Teams?

Yes. It listens to your microphone independently, so it works alongside any calling app, and in person too.

What does connecting my calendar do?

It’s optional. If you connect it, Sound Meter reads your events from the macOS Calendar app to show how loud you were in each meeting. Events are read on your Mac when you open Insights and are never stored or sent anywhere. To include a Google or Outlook calendar, add that account to your Mac’s Calendar app.

Does it pick up sound from my speakers?

It measures whatever your microphone hears, so loud music or a call played through speakers can raise the reading. For the most accurate picture of your own voice, use headphones. You can also turn on Voice Focus (beta) in the orb’s options, which uses macOS voice processing so speaker sound counts less.

Will other people on my call see it?

Only if you share your entire screen. Share a single window or tab and the orb stays between you and your Mac.

It turns red too easily (or never does). Can I adjust it?

Yes. Hover the orb and click Options to choose from five sensitivity levels. Pick the one where your normal speaking voice stays green.

How do I quit or uninstall it?

Click the Sound Meter icon in your menu bar and choose Quit. To uninstall, drag Sound Meter from Applications to the Trash.

Is it really free?

Yes. Free, with no account and no ads. The source code is on GitHub.
